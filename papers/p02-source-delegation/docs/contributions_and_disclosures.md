# Contributions and Disclosures

Fide AI defined the research question, factorial design, preregistration,
estimands, scoring protocol, provider-routing requirements, evidence policy,
analysis, manuscript, and release boundary. Fide AI also controlled study
credentials, confirmatory execution, raw evidence custody, and the decision to
release derived artifacts.

The Apologist Project maintains the shared Scripture-fidelity implementation
used for local execution and collaborated on the broader research direction.
Its implementation remains in its own repository and is not represented as
Fide AI's private evaluation engine. The paper evaluates a research instrument,
not a commercial product, and makes no product endorsement.

Model and infrastructure providers had no role in the study design, analysis,
or manuscript. Provider names identify evaluated endpoints and routes only.

The exact shared implementation revision named in the prospective lock is
publicly reachable at partner commit
[`dbb1514`](https://github.com/apologist-project/llm-scripture-fidelity/commit/dbb1514cde5c7a8e17944d09cf16d0ed1ee619cc).
The public prompt templates and derived data are sufficient to audit the
intervention and reported statistics, but they are not a substitute for
preserving the executed implementation.
