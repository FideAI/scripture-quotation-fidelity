# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository hosts research that touches religious texts and practice.
Contributors and maintainers commit to conduct that is professional, accurate,
and respectful of the communities whose texts are studied here.

Expected behavior:

- Engage with the research on its merits: methodology, evidence, and claims.
- Be precise about religious traditions, texts, and practices. If you are
  unsure, ask rather than assume.
- Respect the claims boundary. This project measures textual delivery; it does
  not adjudicate theology, exegesis, canon, or pastoral judgment.
- Assume good faith across traditions and across disagreement.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about any person or group.
- Disparagement of a religious tradition, denomination, or translation
  community.
- Using this repository to argue for the doctrinal or linguistic superiority of
  any translation. The research does not support such claims and will not host
  them.
- Publishing others' private information, or posting restricted source text,
  credentials, or partner-private material.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, and
to public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to alex@fideai.org. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent consequences as determined by project leadership.
